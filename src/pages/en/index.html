From a point to a solid

A single patent is a point. Points become a line, lines form a plane, and planes stack into a solid. Every company is at a different stage, and each stage calls for a different strategy.

  1. Point

    One patent opens doors: venture certification, government R&D grants, technology guarantees and startup visas.

  2. Line

    Connected patents set the direction of your technology.

  3. Plane

    Lines together cover your business territory.

  4. Solid

    Layered planes reveal the shape of the company.

Strategy before filing

Filing papers alone does not protect a business. Every application goes through four steps.

  1. Understand the core

    We look beyond the technology to the product, the service flow and the business model.

  2. Check design-around and dispute risk

    The goal is a scope of rights you can enforce, not just a registration.

  3. Plan the portfolio

    We set priorities across Korea, the US, Europe, Japan and other markets.

  4. Anticipate examination

    Office action responses, amendments and, if needed, trials are planned from the start.
